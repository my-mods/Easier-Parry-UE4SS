[b]Easier Parry - UE4SS[/b]
More forgiving parries. The held-guard correction remains under investigation after an unresolved in-game report.

[b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*]Doubles the parry window by default. Choose any multiplier from 0.1x to 50x; difficulty adjustments are preserved.
[*]Includes a native correction intended to preserve held guard through attacks and dodges; the reported failure remains unresolved.
[*]Native guard handling uses gameplay events. Optional read-only Lua diagnostics observe guard/input transitions.
[*]Personal timing settings stay separate from the mod and survive updates.
[/list]
Actual guard release and ability cancellation still end guarding. Normal stamina and dodge animation logic remain unchanged. The fix targets guard held before the action. In-game combat and frame-time validation remain pending.

[b]Installation and updates[/b]
Requires [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/thebloodofdawnwalker/mods/18]UE4SS for Dawnwalker[/url]. Install the ZIP through Vortex, select [b]Root (game folder)[/b], then enable and deploy. Both the native assets and Lua timing script are required.

For updates from the older UE4SS package, close the game, disable the old entry and deploy. Replace/reinstall that entry through the installer as Root (game folder), then enable and deploy. Redeployment alone keeps the old installer layout. Keep one enabled entry. Personal settings are not replaced.

[b]Diagnostic build[/b]
guardTraceLogging = true is enabled for this investigation. It records bounded GuardTrace events in ue4ss/UE4SS.log; set false in your personal INI and restart to disable. Reproduce with LT held, release/repress it once after the failure, then retain the log before another launch. No new gameplay correction is claimed.

[b]Settings[/b]
Your personal INI is created on first launch:
[code]%LOCALAPPDATA%/Dawnwalker/Saved/Config/EasierParryUE4SS.ini[/code]
Add overrides under [General], then restart:
[code][General]
factor = 2.0[/code]
Use factor = 1.0 for vanilla timing. Omitted settings inherit shipped defaults.

[b]Native guard fixes are always active while installed.[/b] The old dodgeInterruptsGuard setting is ignored and the dodge console toggle is retired. enabled and easierparry on/off control only timing. Existing personal INI entries are preserved.

UE4SS console shortcuts apply immediately and save timing choices:
[code]easierparry 3
easierparry off
easierparry on
easierparry status[/code]
To uninstall or restore stock guard behavior, disable/remove the mod through Vortex, deploy and restart. Personal settings remain.

[b]Compatibility and source[/b]
Targets Steam build 25129649 / CL-257186. Replaces GA_Input_CombatBlock and GA_Dodge. Other mods replacing either ability, or changing parry timing, may conflict. Controller Tweaks and Remap changes different assets and can remain enabled.
