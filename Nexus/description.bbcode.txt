[b]Easier Parry - UE4SS[/b]
More forgiving parries, with an optional dodge that interrupts guard.

[b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*]Doubles the parry window by default. Choose any multiplier from 0.1x to 50x; difficulty adjustments are preserved.
[*]Dodge can release guard automatically, so you can attempt a dodge while holding guard. Enabled by default; easy to turn off.
[*]Personal settings stay separate from the mod and survive updates.
[/list]
Keep holding guard to resume blocking after a dodge; failed dodge attempts also preserve held guard. Releasing guard cancels recovery. Normal stamina and combat restrictions still apply. Recovery adds no timer or input polling.

[b]Installation[/b]
Requires [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/thebloodofdawnwalker/mods/18]UE4SS for Dawnwalker[/url]. Install the ZIP through Vortex, select [b]UE4SS (Lua mods)[/b] if prompted, then enable and deploy.

For manual installation, copy Data/EasierParryUE4SS from the ZIP into:
[code]The Blood of Dawnwalker/Dawnwalker/Binaries/Win64/ue4ss/Mods[/code]

[b]Settings[/b]
On first launch, the mod creates your personal INI here:
[code]%LOCALAPPDATA%/Dawnwalker/Saved/Config/EasierParryUE4SS.ini[/code]
Add the settings you want to override under [General], then restart the game:
[code][General]
factor = 2.0
dodgeInterruptsGuard = true[/code]
Use factor = 1.0 for vanilla parry timing, or dodgeInterruptsGuard = false to disable guard interruption. The shipped EasierParryUE4SS.defaults.ini supplies any omitted settings; updates do not replace your personal file.

UE4SS console shortcuts apply immediately and save your choice:
[code]easierparry 3
easierparry dodge on
easierparry dodge off[/code]
Use easierparry on/off to enable/disable both features, or easierparry status to check settings. Direct INI edits require a restart.

[b]Updating and removing[/b]
Before upgrading from an older version, copy Scripts/EasierParryUE4SS.ini from the old mod to the personal path above if you have custom settings and no personal file yet. Close the game, replace/reinstall the same Vortex entry, and deploy. Keep one enabled entry.

To uninstall, remove the mod through Vortex and deploy, or delete only its EasierParryUE4SS folder for a manual install. Personal settings remain.

[b]Compatibility and source[/b]
No game assets are replaced. Other mods changing parry timing or guard/dodge behavior may conflict.
