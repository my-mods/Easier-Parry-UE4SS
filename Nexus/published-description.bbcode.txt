[size=5][b][color=#E8C88A]Easier Parry and Dodge While Blocking[/color][/b][/size]

More forgiving parries, with native held-guard recovery after dodging. Attack inputs retain the base game’s guard behavior.

[size=4][b][color=#E8C88A]Features[/color][/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Doubles the parry window by default. Choose any multiplier from 0.1x to 50x; difficulty adjustments are preserved.[/*]
[*]Held guard resumes after native dodge suppression ends, unless an attack or actual guard release has ended guarding.[/*]
[*]Personal settings stay separate from the mod and survive updates.[/*]
[/list]
Normal dodge eligibility, stamina and animation rules remain in effect. Stock attack/combo transitions can still end guard; a later dodge does not reactivate an ended guard ability. Release and press guard again in that case.

Requires [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/thebloodofdawnwalker/mods/18]UE4SS for Dawnwalker[/url].

[size=4][b][color=#E8C88A]Installation[/color][/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Vortex:[/color][/b] Install Easier-Parry-and-Dodge-While-Blocking.zip through Vortex, enable it and deploy.
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Manual:[/color][/b] Copy the archive's Dawnwalker folder into ...\steamapps\common\The Blood of Dawnwalker\, preserving the folder structure.
[/list]

[size=4][b][color=#E8C88A]Settings[/color][/b][/size]

On first launch, the mod creates your personal INI here:
[code]%LOCALAPPDATA%/Dawnwalker/Saved/Config/EasierParryUE4SS.ini[/code]
Add the settings you want to override under [General], then restart the game:
[code][General]
factor = 2.0[/code]
Use factor = 1.0 for vanilla parry timing.

UE4SS console shortcuts apply immediately and save your choice:
[code]easierparry 3
easierparry off
easierparry on[/code]
Use easierparry on/off to enable/disable only the timing multiplier, or easierparry status to check settings. Direct INI edits require a restart.
